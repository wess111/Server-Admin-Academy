How the academy works

A simple flow that matches real IT work. Clear steps help students understand what to do next. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Pick a track
HelpDesk, System Administration, or Security—aligned to your course outcomes.

Open a lab ticket
Students work enterprise-style tickets (symptom → root cause → remediation).
Step 03
Document the change

Step 04
