The Restarting Computer

A Windows laptop repeatedly blue-screens and restarts every 30–60 minutes. IT installed system and driver updates, but the crashes continued. Your team has been assigned the incident.

Previous action: IT installed available Windows and driver updates. The device was returned to the user. The issue returned.

Your task: Determine the probable root cause, take the most appropriate corrective action, and verify system stability.
